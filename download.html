First launch

Expect a warning the first time you open it

Early builds are not yet signed, so Mac and Windows both warn you before opening Ascent Mapper. You only have to get past this once.

On a Mac

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Ascent Mapper into Applications.
  2. Open Ascent Mapper from Applications. macOS says it could not verify the app; click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about Ascent Mapper, and click Open Anyway. Confirm with your password, then click Open.

On macOS 14 or earlier you can instead Control-click the app in Applications, choose Open, then Open again.

On Windows

  1. If your browser says the file isn't commonly downloaded, choose to keep it.
  2. Run the installer. If Windows protected your PC appears, click More info, then Run anyway.

Ascent Mapper installs for your account only, without needing administrator rights.

School- or work-managed computers may block unsigned apps entirely. If yours does, ask your IT team.
