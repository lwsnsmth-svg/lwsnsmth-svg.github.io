Evaluation Process

A four-phase evaluation pipeline. Our process is recursive, building upon past findings and expanding a model's explanatory leverage.

Opening the black box

A platform built for program teams that gets to outcomes by providing clarity in practice.

Phase 01

Evaluative Foundation

The conceptual infrastructure that makes rigorous evaluation possible: stakeholder map, logic model, theory of change.

Phase 02

Data Architecture

A practical system for collecting and managing data while preserving the needs of stakeholder communities.

Phase 03

Analytics & Inference

Collected data turned into defensible evidence; outcomes here inform change recommendations and help scope further inquiry.

Phase 04

Monitoring, Learning & Reporting

Findings converted into systems that sustain learning and accountability over time.

Our program

The process is the program.

Reports and communication support are deliverables, but the program team's active part in pursuing inquiry, scoping analysis and adopting new methods is central to the process.

The implementation support professional coordinates the evaluation, shepherds findings into program changes, and helps build a culture of inquiry within the team. The organization's staff co-create the frameworks, collection methods, pilots and data architecture alongside them.

The sequence runs in one direction until it reaches recursion and integration, and then it loops: findings become program changes, outcomes feed the next round of evaluation, and the cycle repeats until graduation.

  1. Step 01

    Intake

    A scoping conversation and intake assessment establish whether the program is a fit and what existing systems can be built upon.

  2. Step 02

    Embed

    The implementation support professional joins the program team; goals and timelines are agreed together.

  3. Step 03

    Audit

    A structured review of the frameworks, data and systems already in place, so nothing that works gets rebuilt.

  4. Step 04

    Evaluation

    Phases 1 to 3 of the pipeline, co-created with the organization's staff.

  5. Step 05

    Communication

    What the evaluation found, turned into material for the community, the board and funders.

  6. Step 06

    Recursion and integration

    Traces the underlying links that turn inputs to outcomes: findings become program changes, outcomes feed the next round of evaluation, and the loop cycles.

  7. Step 07

    Graduation

    After approximately two years, embedded support draws back under a written handoff plan agreed with the program team.

  8. Step 08

    Onward support

    Continue on a discounted monitoring retainer or take a written implementation guide. Our internal research, analysis and networks remain available.
