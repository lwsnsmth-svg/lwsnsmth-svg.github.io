Using the app

Six views of one program

Stakeholder map

Who is affected by the program and how they are tied to one another.

Logic model

Map the connections that take program inputs through activities and outputs to short-, medium- and long-term outcomes. Place each logical pathway on its own layer and aggregate them to visualize the logical trusses of your program.

Theory of change

The assumptions each causal link depends on, and the evidence for each one. An automatic research tool uses your model structure to search research databases for relevant studies, shows you what links they relate to, and rates their evidence strength.

Policy

The rules regulating the program today, the changes in development, and which of the program's measures each one affects.

Change

Outputs and outcomes modeled dynamically and projected under changes to inputs and policy, with every scenario read against a baseline.

Data

Every number in the model, held once as a series and traced to each place it is used.
